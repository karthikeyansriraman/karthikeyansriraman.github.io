What is OneVault?

OneVault keeps every part of your life that happens on a date, and answers one question when you open it: what needs me today?

Insurance renewals. Medicine refills. Passport expiry. Property tax. The car service. Your sister's birthday. Past due items come first and largest; this week next, then this month. Nothing else competes for your eye.

Tap "Mark as done" and OneVault records what happened — the date, the amount, a note, a receipt — then moves the reminder forward one cycle. Your history builds itself.

And for the things that have no date at all — WiFi passwords, blood groups, locker combinations — there are Notes. Titles only in the list, never a preview, because that is where people keep passwords.

How pricing works

OneVault is free for 30 days, with everything working.

After that, you keep full use of everything you have already added — marking things done, editing, attaching files, reminders, search, all of it. Only adding new items, categories and notes needs the one-time purchase.

Nothing is ever hidden or deleted because a trial ended. There is no subscription, and there never will be.
